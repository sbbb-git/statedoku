    How to play

    • Fill the nine cells with US states. Each state has to fit both its row clue and its column clue.
    • A state can be used only once in the grid.
    • Any state that fits both clues counts, and you can change or clear a cell at any time.
    • You have five mistakes.
    • A clue with a ? has an explanation: tap it.
    • A new grid every day at midnight. Past grids stay in the archive.

    Example: row One of the 13 colonies, column Atlantic coast: Virginia fits both. Pennsylvania was a colony but has no ocean coast; Florida is on the Atlantic but was never a colony.
