Puzzle: stop refusing correct answers. Plus site audit fixes and a 600-tweet bank.

THE PUZZLE FIX

The game refused answers that satisfied both visible clues. The owner hit it,
and it turns out to be the central rule rather than an edge case.

js/game.js ran _isStillSolvable on a trial grid and rejected any placement that
made the grid unsolvable, with a toast saying the answer conflicted elsewhere.
That message could never be true. puzzle.js:348 only ships grids with a unique
solution, so "keeps the grid solvable" and "equals the canonical answer" are
mathematically the same test. The game was saying "you did not pick my answer"
to players who had reasoned correctly.

Measured over 365 generated puzzles:
  97.8% of days contain at least one cell where a correct answer is refused
  28.5% of all cells accept more than one clue-satisfying state
  24.9% of all valid answers get refused
  53.8% of games hit a refusal when filling cells left to right
   0.0% when filling most-constrained-cell first

That last pair is the damning one. Identical knowledge was rewarded or punished
by turn order alone.

Reproduction, today's grid: "Starts with M" x "Mountain timezone" accepts only
Montana, and "Starts with M" x "Name ends in A" accepts Minnesota or Montana.
Playing Montana in the second cell is correct by every visible clue and was
silently rejected, cell left empty, score unchanged.

Fixed by deleting the gate and making "satisfies both clues" the definition of
correct everywhere: _updateScore, _renderCells, the cell lock, the share emoji
grid, the share text, the share image, and critically the reload scrub in
_loadProgress, which would otherwise have wiped every valid non-canonical
placement on refresh. _checkSolved already accepted any valid grid of nine
distinct states as a win, with a comment saying so, so the rule the code
already believed in can finally apply.

Accepting every valid answer means a player can spend a state another cell
needed, so filled cells are now editable and the picker has a Clear button.
Without that the fix would have traded "punished for being right" for "stuck
with no way out". Cells stay keyboard reachable, the not-allowed cursor is
gone, and the aria-label says the cell can be changed rather than that it is
locked. The two conflict strings are deleted from all three languages so
nobody wires them back up.

Verified in a browser end to end: Montana accepted, cell reopens, Clear empties
it and returns the score to 0/9, Minnesota accepted in its place, and the
placement survives a reload.

PUZZLE UI

The onboarding tooltip sat at top: 70px, directly over the column header row,
completely hiding the middle constraint. It now sits below the grid with the
arrow pointing up. Verified: zero header overlap.

The state picker is a bottom sheet, which is right on a phone and wrong on a
desktop, where it opened roughly 400px below the cell being filled. On screens
at least 580px wide it is now positioned from the grid's measured geometry:
below the grid when there is room, above it when there is not, falling back to
the sheet on short viewports. Verified against real page geometry at 900, 768,
650 and 500px.

SITE AUDIT FIXES

Six parallel audits of the live site produced these, all verified before fixing:

- Created 404.html. Every nonexistent URL was returning 200 with the full
  homepage, because Cloudflare Pages falls back to SPA mode without one. That
  is an unbounded set of duplicate 200-status URLs during a low-value-content
  review, and it silently masked every broken link on the site.
- 588 Spanish and French pages had rel=canonical pointing at their English
  equivalent while also declaring themselves the es/fr hreflang alternate. The
  two signals cancel: Google honours the canonical and drops the translation.
- 262 pages carried FAQPage structured data whose questions appeared nowhere on
  the page. Markup-only FAQ is what draws a spammy-structured-markup manual
  action. The answers already existed in the JSON-LD, so they are now rendered
  as a real accordion, 1,192 questions across those pages. Zero pages remain in
  breach.
- 375 em-dashes survived inside JSON-LD because the earlier strip skipped
  script blocks. That text is exactly what Google renders in rich results.
  Fixed by parsing and re-serialising each block, so all 3,729 still validate.
- 48 titles had been cut mid-word by an over-eager 62-character trim, leaving
  half-words and unclosed parentheses. Recovered from og:title where available
  and re-trimmed at word boundaries, never ending on a dangling article.
- 168 links from Spanish and French pages pointed at English slugs inside their
  own language tree. 41 hreflang alternates pointed at files that do not exist.
- 19 meta descriptions had boilerplate spliced mid-word at apostrophes.
- 4 pages referenced /styles/learn.css, a path that has never existed, so they
  rendered with no site stylesheet at all.
- One title contained a raw unclosed anchor tag injected by an auto-linker.
- /launch/ now 301s to the homepage. It was the pre-launch teaser, 92 words,
  still indexed two months after launch.
- robots.txt disallows /tmp/, the www to apex redirect is uncommented, and the
  duplicated /play/ CSP block is removed from _headers so the middleware is the
  single source. Emitting it twice made browsers enforce the stricter of the
  two, which is what would break the embeddable widgets.

TWITTER BOT

Replaced the runtime Anthropic call with a pre-generated bank of 600 tweets at
data/tweets.json, written in one pass across 12 angles of 50, interleaved so no
two consecutive posts share a theme. 600 unique, max 237 characters, validated
against the voice rules. The bank covers 300 days at two posts a day and wraps
rather than failing.

X moved off fixed Basic plans to pay-per-use in 2026 at 0.015 USD per post and
0.20 USD if the post contains a link, so SKIP_LINK_TWEETS lets the cost be
dialled from about 3.50 USD a month down to 0.70. The dead prompt-building and
Anthropic code is removed, 498 lines down to 235. humanize() stays as a
backstop on the bank and on manual ?text= posts.

DATA

overtook Columbia at the 2020 census, so the count of states where the capital
is also the largest city is 17, not 18. Not a puzzle constraint, so no gameplay
