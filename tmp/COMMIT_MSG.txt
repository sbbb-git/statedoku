Stop humanize() flattening the line break before the link

humanize() ended on replace(/\s+/g, ' '), which collapses newlines along with
spaces. The bank deliberately puts the link on its own line after a blank one,
and that final sweep undid it, so every post went out with the URL welded to the
end of the last sentence:

  ... still the sixth largest state in the union. #Statedoku https://statedoku.com/states/illinois/

Caught in the dry run against the live worker, not in the bank, because the bank
stores the newlines correctly and only the scrub destroys them.

Now collapses horizontal whitespace only, tidies spaces around a break, and
caps runs of blank lines at one. The dash, caps and opener rules are unchanged.

Deployed, version 3ba18b2f.
