Ahrefs audit part two: no page is orphaned any more

34 indexable pages had no incoming internal link at all. An orphan gets almost
no crawl priority and receives no internal PageRank, so the work that went into
writing them was being thrown away.

18 of them were the substantial World Cup pages, orphaned for a simple reason:
/learn/ had no World Cup section. Not a broken link, a missing one. The other
notable pair was /learn/most-populous-states/ and /learn/national-parks-by-state/,
evergreen pages nothing pointed at.

Each orphan is now linked from its own language hub, in the card markup those
hubs already use so the blocks do not read as bolted on:

  /learn/      +14 World Cup, +3 other
  /es/learn/    +6 World Cup, +4 other
  /fr/learn/    +3 World Cup

The four outside a learn tree got the link that belongs to them rather than a
hub dump: each /play/<game>/guide/ is now linked from its own game page, and
/press/ from /about/, which is where a journalist actually looks.

Verified in a browser at 375px: the new sections render in the existing card
style and the page does not scroll horizontally.

Also swept the structured data for the four schema.org errors the crawl
reported. Checked every Article, BlogPosting, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, ItemList,
Game, WebSite and VideoGame node across 3,728 blocks for missing required
properties and over-long headlines: none found. Whatever Ahrefs saw on 11 August
either predates the last push or needs their validator to reproduce, so it is
left alone rather than guessed at.

Local audit now reads zero on every counter it can measure: hreflang return
tags, orphans, broken internal links, meta descriptions too long, too short or
missing, and incomplete Open Graph.
