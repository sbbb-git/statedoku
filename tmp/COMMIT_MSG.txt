Ahrefs audit: kill the hreflang errors, the broken links and the spliced descriptions

Reproduced the locally checkable half of the 11 August crawl against the repo,
since that crawl predates the last push and fixing from a five-day-old report
means fixing things that may no longer be true.

HREFLANG, THE TOP ERROR

114 declarations across 43 pages had no return tag, every one of them a French
or Spanish page declaring an English alternate that the English page never
declared back.

Repaired pairwise rather than by rebuilding clusters. A union-find over the
hreflang graph produces clusters that already contain two pages of the same
language, because /learn/state-capitals/ and /learn/states-and-capitals/ are
distinct pages that reference each other. Writing a cluster's full alternate set
into every member would have made two pages claim hreflang="es" in one set,
which is invalid and worse than the current state.

So: add the missing return tag where the language slot is free, and drop the
forward claim where another page already holds it. 50 return tags added, 20
forward claims dropped. Reciprocity failures now 0.

BROKEN INTERNAL LINKS

217 links to paths that do not exist, over 134 pages. 182 of them were two
targets: /es/learn/us-regions/ and /es/learn/state-flags/, English slugs inside
the Spanish tree, left over from the translation pass. The pages exist, at
regiones-de-eeuu and banderas-de-estados.

The rest were repointed where a real destination existed, or unwrapped where
none did. District of Columbia is not a state and gets no /states/ page, so
those anchors are now plain text. Eleven Spanish capital pages linked from a
sibling were never written; creating eleven thin pages during an AdSense review
is a worse trade than removing eleven links. Now 0.

CANONICALS THAT DELETED THEIR OWN PAGE

Nine pages carrying real translated content, 550 to 600 words with lang="fr" or
lang="es", declared rel=canonical pointing at the English version. Google honours
the canonical and drops the translation, so those pages could never rank. This
was fixed for 588 pages earlier; these nine were missed.

Two were worse: /es/learn/regiones-de-eeuu/ and /es/learn/banderas-de-estados/
pointed their canonical at /es/learn/us-regions/ and /es/learn/state-flags/,
which do not exist. Those are the pages 182 links were just repointed to, so
they had to be self-canonical for the link fix to mean anything.

SITEMAP

61 noindexed URLs were being submitted, which Search Console reports as
"Submitted URL marked noindex". The generator is a Pages Function assembled from
hardcoded entries and cannot read the filesystem at request time, so the entries
are removed at source. 63 lines gone, two of them duplicates. Also dropped
colonias-originales, which correctly canonicalises to 13-colonies.

The generator's 271 literal paths now resolve to a real file, none noindexed,
none non-canonical.

META DESCRIPTIONS

Twelve English descriptions had boilerplate spliced into the middle of a word:

  Every US state. Full 50-state breakdown with data, maps, and updates for 2026.'s 2-letter postal code

An earlier expansion matched attribute content with [^"']*, which stops at the
first apostrophe. It captured up to "state", appended a full stop and the
boilerplate, and left the rest dangling after it. The French copy hit this and
was fixed; the English pages were missed because the same naive regex was used
to find them. og:description escaped the splice and was the recovery source for
seven; five were rewritten from the page's own h1 and opening paragraph.

Three more were cut mid-list at an apostrophe, "Texas 'Lone Star'" losing
everything from the quote. Four ended on an unclosed parenthesis. All rewritten.
Eleven over 160 characters trimmed at a sentence or word boundary.

Descriptions now: 0 too long, 0 too short, 0 missing, 0 unbalanced.

Three widget pages had no og:image and now share the site image.

Verified across 2,088 pages: no broken head, no duplicate canonical, no
duplicate hreflang, 3,728 JSON-LD blocks all still parsing.
