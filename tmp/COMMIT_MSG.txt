Deep SEO pass on Aug 7 data: rewrite 250 subtopic pages, 38 new pages, fix the uniqueness problem properly

Covers everything since af5a6f0e. The earlier Aug 7 commit never landed, git
add stalled on 1,300 files while agents were writing concurrently, so this
one carries that work too.

THE ACTUAL PROBLEM, AND HOW IT WAS DIAGNOSED WRONG TWICE

AdSense flagged the site for low-value content. The first response was to
noindex 813 doorway launchers, which was right. The second was to noindex all
600 /states/<state>/<subtopic>/ pages, which was too broad. The third was to
restore 250 of them because they were long, which was wrong for a different
reason: length is not the test.

Measuring median pairwise 5-gram overlap across all 50 states per subtopic
shows what was actually happening:

    history    1063 words   61.7% overlap
    geography   842 words   59.1%
    people      880 words   62.5%

Long pages, generated from a template with the state name swapped in. That is
the pattern Google penalises, and no amount of word count fixes it.

WHAT CHANGED

All five subtopics with demand were rewritten so each page is genuinely about
its own state. Measured after:

    geography  59.1% -> 4.0%     symbols   59% -> 7.9%
    people     62.5% -> 3.9%     fun-facts ~55% -> 7.5%
    history    61.7% -> 3.6%

Minnesota geography, the single most-cited page on the site at 987 AI
citations plus 797 impressions at position 6.6, now opens on the Northwest
Angle: 123 square miles unreachable without crossing into Manitoba, the
northernmost land in the contiguous 48, a border post that is a shed with a
videophone, all of it caused by an error in the 1783 Treaty of Paris traced
back to John Mitchell's 1755 map. That is what these pages should have been.

map and sports stay noindexed permanently. No citation evidence, and the
state hub already serves the intent. economy, food, weather, travel and
elections stay out too, 138-237 words with no demand worth the rewrite.

NEW PAGES, ALL EVIDENCE-BACKED

38 pages, each justified by a query in the Aug 7 exports rather than by
completing a set. Generating all 50 abbreviation codes per language would
have repeated the doorway mistake, so only codes with individual demand got
a page:

  18 EN /learn/what-state-is-<code>/     "what state is mo" 405 impressions
                                         at position 5.3, "is pa pennsylvania
                                         or philadelphia" 277 AI citations
                                         at 44.5% share
  12 ES /es/learn/estado-eeuu-abreviatura-<code>/
   6 ES /es/learn/<city>-es-un-estado/   "is miami a state" 141 citations
   1 FR /fr/learn/liste-des-50-etats/    100% AI citation share, no page
   1 ES /es/learn/lista-de-los-50-estados/   82.3% share, no page

Each abbreviation page is built on its own code's collision history rather
than a template. MD lost MA to Massachusetts inside the eight-state M block
and fell back to the Md. dateline form. TX is first-and-last because
Tennessee took TE. NV lost NE to Nebraska. Measured overlap between siblings
is 0.4 to 4.5 percent.

STATEHOOD ANSWERS AND THE 44K QUERY

"estadidad de wyoming" is the largest single query on the site: 44,449
impressions at position 3.6 with 16 clicks, a 0.04% CTR, and separately the
top AI grounding query at 1,631 citations. The page ranked and never answered
the question. All 50 Spanish state pages now carry a direct-answer statehood
block with the exact admission date, statehood number and prior territorial
status, plus a matching FAQPage entry. Wyoming's title and description were
rewritten around statehood, the other 49 keep theirs since the data does not
show statehood as their dominant intent.

REFERENCE CLUSTER

/learn/states-and-capitals/ 944 -> 1,649 words with ItemList JSON-LD over all
50 states, a statehood-order table and an abbreviation grid.
/learn/13-colonies/ 687 -> 960 words with ItemList and a section answering
"which states were NOT among the 13", a query the page never served.

Both already hold 91-100% AI citation share on their query cluster while
ranking 5-9 in search. The gap was structure, not authority.

HOUSEKEEPING

- Sitemap 634 -> 920 URLs, now deduped at source. Eight duplicate <loc>
  entries existed, two of them predating this work.
- /launch/ 301s to the homepage. It was the pre-launch teaser, still indexed
  two months after launch, 92 words, titled "Launches Monday June 1".
- states/oklahoma/elections/ and states/alaska/weather/ had escaped the
  noindex sweep. Caught by a new filesystem-to-sitemap reconciliation script.
- 3,155 em-dashes stripped across 637 files. Agents do not hold that rule
  reliably, so it is enforced by a deterministic pass instead.

TOOLING ADDED

tmp/reindex-expanded.py now gates on two conditions, not one: a page needs
600+ words AND its topic must measure under 25% median overlap. The topic
gate is what would have prevented the wrong call earlier, since it rejects a
whole subtopic on similarity even when every page is long enough.

tmp/sitemap-audit.py reconciles the filesystem against the served sitemap in
both directions and exits non-zero on any discrepancy.
