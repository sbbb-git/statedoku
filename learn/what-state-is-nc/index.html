What state is NC?

NC is North Carolina. It is the two-letter USPS code for the state of North Carolina, whose capital city is Raleigh.

NC belongs to the most crowded corner of the postal system. Eight state codes begin with the letter N, matched only by the eight that begin with M, so the character after the N is doing all of the identifying work.

Why the code is NC

When two-letter codes were fixed in October 1963, three months after ZIP codes began, the rule for a two-word state name was to take the initial of each word. North Carolina yields N and C. Nothing pulled from inside the word North would have worked, because North Dakota would have produced the same letters.

That rule solves the whole N block, which is where the design pressure sat. NC and ND take their second letter from the second word. NH, NJ, NM and NY do the same, except that in those four the N stands for New rather than North. Nevada, a single word, takes N and the V from the third position. Nebraska is the only N state given the plain first two letters, and even that was not settled at once: the original 1963 list printed Nebraska as NB, and the Postal Service revised it to NE in 1969 after NB proved to clash with New Brunswick on cross-border mail.

North Carolina itself needed no revision. It had been set in print as N.C. for generations, and the reform removed the periods and left the letters alone.

Quick facts about North Carolina

USPS code: NC

Capital: Raleigh

Largest city: Charlotte

Region: South, on the Atlantic coast

Statehood: 1789, one of the original thirteen

Population band: over 10 million

North Carolina ratified the Constitution in 1789, after the new government had already begun operating, which makes it one of the last of the original thirteen to join. Its population now passes ten million, putting it in the top band with nine other states.

Where you will see NC

  • Mail. Charlotte NC or Raleigh NC on the closing line, two capitals, no periods, ZIP to the right.
  • License plates. North Carolina plates carry the slogan First in Flight, a reference to the Wright brothers' 1903 flights on the Outer Banks. Ohio has contested the claim for decades.
  • Pro sports. The state's two big franchises drop the direction entirely: the Carolina Panthers in the NFL, based in Charlotte, and the Carolina Hurricanes in the NHL, based in Raleigh.
  • College sports. NC State keeps the postal code in its name, while the University of North Carolina goes by UNC, so both letters and word order flip depending on the school.
  • Crosswords. "Tar Heel State: abbr." is the usual clue for NC.
  • Data fields. The international form is US-NC. Alone in a country list, NC is New Caledonia.
Fastest check against South Carolina: Raleigh, Charlotte and Asheville are NC. Columbia, Charleston and Myrtle Beach are SC.

Common questions

Is NC North Carolina or North Dakota?

NC is North Carolina. North Dakota is ND.

How many state codes start with N?

Eight: NC, ND, NE, NH, NJ, NM, NV and NY.

What is the capital of North Carolina?

Raleigh. The largest city is Charlotte.

Did any N code change after 1963?

Nebraska was NB on the first list and became NE in 1969 to avoid a clash with New Brunswick.
