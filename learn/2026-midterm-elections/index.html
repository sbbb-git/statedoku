2026 Midterm Elections: Results by State

Senate, governors and the House, state by state, from the vote of 3 November 2026.

Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate in the midterm elections of 3 November 2026, according to Associated Press projections.

In the Senate, AP has called 32 of the 35 races: 20 won by Democrats and 12 by Republicans. 7 of them changed party, all to Democrats. With the 65 seats that were not on the ballot, the Democratic caucus has 54 seats and Republicans 43, with 3 races still to be called.

Of the 36 governor races, AP has called 31: 19 won by Democrats and 12 by Republicans, and 4 governorships changed party, all to Democrats. In the House, AP has called 219 seats for Democrats and 135 for Republicans; 81 are still being counted, and a majority is 218.

The 35 Senate races

One third of the Senate is elected every two years. In 2026 the 33 seats of class 2 were up, plus two special elections, in Ohio and Florida, for the seats JD Vance and Marco Rubio left in January 2025. Before the vote Republicans held 53 seats and Democrats 47, 2 of them independents who caucus with the Democrats. With the vice president breaking ties, Democrats needed a net gain of 4 seats to take the majority.

The House of Representatives

All 435 seats were elected, as in every even year. How many each state holds was set by the 2020 census, from 52 for California to one for each of the six least populated states (see House seats by state since 1910). A majority is 218 seats. AP has projected that Democrats won the majority.

Why midterms usually go against the president

In the 23 midterm elections from 1934 to 2022, the president's party gained House seats only three times: in 1934, 1998 and 2002 (American Presidency Project, University of California, Santa Barbara). In every other one it lost seats. In 2026 the president, Donald Trump, is a Republican, and Republicans held both the House and the Senate before the vote.

What happens next

States count the remaining ballots, then certify their results over the following weeks, each on its own calendar. Close races can go to a recount. The new Congress is sworn in on 3 January 2027, and most new governors take office in January.

Frequently asked questions

Who won the 2026 midterm elections?

Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate in the midterm elections of 3 November 2026, according to Associated Press projections. AP has called 32 of the 35 Senate races and 31 of the 36 governor races; the rest are still being counted.

When were the 2026 midterms?

On Tuesday 3 November 2026. Many states opened early or mail voting weeks before.

How many Senate seats were up in 2026?

35: the 33 seats of class 2 and special elections in Ohio and Florida, for the seats JD Vance and Marco Rubio left in January 2025.

How many governors were elected in 2026?

Thirty-six, in 36 states, plus the governors of three territories: Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands and the US Virgin Islands.

What is a midterm election?

The federal election held halfway through a president's four-year term. All 435 House seats, about a third of the Senate and most governors are elected.

When does the new Congress start?

At noon on 3 January 2027, as the 20th Amendment sets for every new Congress.
