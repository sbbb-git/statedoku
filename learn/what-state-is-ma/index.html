What state is MA?

The state that holds the lowest ZIP code in the country, and the two-letter code it nearly did not get.

MA is Massachusetts. It is the USPS two-letter code for the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, where Boston serves as both capital and largest city.

Why the code is MA

The 1963 standardization had one absolute requirement: fifty codes, two characters each, no duplicates. Eight state names start with M, and that constraint is what bent most of them out of shape.

Three of the eight begin with the letters M and A: Maine, Maryland and Massachusetts. Only Massachusetts came out with the plain first-two-letters code. Maine and Maryland were not given near-misses, they were given a completely different construction, first letter plus last letter. MainE produced ME and MarylanD produced MD. That is why neither code resembles the sound of its state name, and why people who learn the first-two-letters rule get both of them wrong.

Massachusetts could not have used that construction anyway. The name ends in S, so first-and-last would have produced MS, and MS is Mississippi. The awkwardness of ME and MD is the direct cost of keeping MA clean.

Before the two-letter era the state was printed as Mass., a form that survived in datelines, case citations and postal directories for well over a century. It remains the version crossword setters reach for.

Quick facts about Massachusetts

  • Capital: Boston
  • Largest city: Boston, one of the minority of states where capital and largest city are the same place
  • Region: Northeast, in New England
  • Statehood: February 6, 1788, the 6th state to ratify the Constitution, and one of the original thirteen colonies
  • Population band: 5 to 10 million
  • Land area: 44th of 50, making it the seventh smallest state
  • Time zone: Eastern

Where you will see MA

Mail. Massachusetts holds the lowest ZIP codes in the United States. The national numbering opens at 01001 in Agawam and the state fills the 010 to 027 range, with Boston in 021 and 022. If a spreadsheet has stripped the leading zero from a five-digit ZIP, the record is almost certainly a Massachusetts one.

License plates. Standard Massachusetts plates spell the state name out and carry the slogan The Spirit of America. The two-letter code lives on the registration and title paperwork rather than the plate itself.

Sports. Boston's three downtown franchises all shorten to BOS. The Patriots use NE. Between them, the state's own code turns up less often on a scoreboard than in a shipping database.

Crosswords. Clues for the Bay State want MASS, four letters, rather than the postal pair.

Forms and databases. MA is the ISO country code for Morocco and the standard shorthand for a Master of Arts. A bare MA in an unlabeled field is ambiguous three ways, which is a real cause of misrouted mail and mismatched records.

Keeping the three apart: Massachusetts is the big one, so it takes the obvious code. Maine and Maryland both hand you their last letter, E and D.
