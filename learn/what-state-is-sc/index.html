What state is SC?

SC is South Carolina. It is the two-letter USPS code for the state of South Carolina, whose capital city is Columbia.

South Carolina sits on the Atlantic coast between North Carolina and Georgia, and it has been written as S.C. in print since colonial times. The code you type into a shipping form today is a 1963 clean-up of a much older habit.

Why the code is SC

ZIP codes went live on 1 July 1963. To make the bottom line of an address readable by sorting equipment, the Post Office Department (the US Postal Service since 1971) needed city, state and ZIP to fit a fixed width, so in October 1963 it published a list that capped every state at two capital letters and no periods.

South Carolina's name is two words, so the code takes one initial from each: S from South, C from Carolina. All ten two-word states were solved that way, which is why NC, ND, SD, NH, NJ, NM, NY, RI and WV read as pairs of initials instead of as clipped single words.

SC itself was never in dispute. The old form, S.C., had been standard in atlases, court citations and newspaper datelines for well over a century, so the reform amounted to deleting two full stops. Single-word states had the harder ride. Massachusetts had to abandon Mass. for MA, and Nebraska shipped in the October 1963 list as NB before the Postal Service changed it to NE in 1969, because NB collided with New Brunswick on Canadian mail.

Quick facts about South Carolina

USPS code: SC

Capital: Columbia

Largest city: Columbia, one of the minority of states where the capital is also the biggest city

Region: South, on the Atlantic coast

Statehood: 1788, one of the original thirteen

Population band: 5 to 10 million

Charleston is the name most visitors know, which is why the capital question trips people up. Columbia sits inland in the middle of the state and outranks Charleston on city population.

Where you will see SC

  • Mail. SC belongs on the final line of an address, after the city and before the ZIP, in capitals with no periods.
  • College sports. USC means the University of South Carolina inside the state and the University of Southern California everywhere else, so national broadcasts label the Gamecocks as South Carolina to keep the two apart.
  • Pro sports. No NFL, NBA, MLB or NHL franchise carries SC. The nearest teams brand themselves Carolina, which covers both states at once.
  • Crosswords. A clue reading "Palmetto State: abbr." is asking for SC. The palmetto is on the state flag.
  • Data fields. The international form is US-SC. On its own, SC is the ISO country code for the Seychelles, which is why a few overseas checkout forms reject it in a country box.

Common questions

Is SC South Carolina or South Dakota?

SC is South Carolina. South Dakota is SD.

What is the capital of South Carolina?

Columbia, which is also the largest city in the state.

Why is South Carolina not just S?

A lone S would not separate it from South Dakota. Every state was fixed at two characters in 1963 so machines could read the address line.

Was South Carolina abbreviated differently before 1963?

It was printed as S.C. with periods. The 1963 reform removed the punctuation and kept the letters.
