About Statedoku

Statedoku is a free daily geography puzzle about the 50 US states: a 3 by 3 grid where each state must match a row clue and a column clue. The site also has 21 mini-games and about 180 articles in English, French and Spanish, and posts a short video every day on TikTok, YouTube and X. It is built by one developer, the founder, who works from France, six hours ahead of New York. Its players are puzzle fans, students and teachers, many of them in the United States.
