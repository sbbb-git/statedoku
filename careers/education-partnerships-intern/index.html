Education Partnerships Intern

Bring Statedoku to teachers, schools, libraries and homeschool groups as a free way to learn the 50 states.

This is a paid, fully remote internship for students and recent graduates living in New York State.

  • Pay: $20 per hour before tax, about $1,730 a month at 20 hours a week, paid monthly through Deel
  • Hours: 20 hours a week, on your own schedule, plus one video call a week
  • Dates: January to June 2027 (six months)
  • Location: Fully remote, for candidates living in New York State
  • Status: Paid internship, as an independent contractor
  • Apply by: December 20, 2026

About Statedoku

Statedoku is a free daily geography puzzle about the 50 US states: a 3 by 3 grid where each state must match a row clue and a column clue. The site also has 21 mini-games and about 180 articles across English, French and Spanish, and posts short videos on TikTok, YouTube and X. It is built by one developer, the founder, who works from France, six hours ahead of New York. Its players are puzzle fans, students and teachers, many of them in the United States.

What you will do

  • Reach educators. Contact social studies and geography teachers, school librarians, homeschool groups and geography clubs, and present Statedoku as a free classroom resource.
  • Build a teacher kit. With the founder, write a short kit: lesson ideas that use the daily puzzle and the mini-games, and how to share them with a class.
  • Listen. Collect what teachers and students say, and turn it into clear requests for the game and the articles.
  • Join the communities. Take part in online teacher communities and events, and keep a list of the ones worth our time.

Who we are looking for

  • You study education, geography, history, social studies or a related field.
  • Teaching, tutoring or camp experience is a plus.
  • You are a college student, undergraduate or graduate, or you graduated in 2025 or 2026.
  • You live in New York State and are authorized to work in the United States.
  • You write clear English and you finish what you start without being chased.
  • You like maps, geography or puzzles. You do not need to know every state capital.

How you will work

  • The job is fully remote. You work 20 hours a week, when it suits you.
  • You meet the founder on one video call a week, in the New York morning, and write a short note every Friday on what you did.
  • You are engaged as an independent contractor by the founder of Statedoku and paid $20 an hour, before tax, each month through Deel, a payroll platform that handles the contract and the payments.

What you will learn

How schools and teachers choose the tools they use, and how to get a free educational product adopted in classrooms.

How to apply

  • Send the form on this page by December 20, 2026, with your résumé as a PDF and five lines answering the question below.
  • If your profile fits, a 30-minute video call with the founder before the end of December.
  • A short task linked to the role, one hour at most.
  • An answer within a week of the call, and a start in January 2027.

Your five lines: Tell us how a teacher you know could use Statedoku with a class.

We welcome applicants of every background. We never ask for a photo, an age or a date of birth. Your application is used only to assess it and is deleted within three months after the internships are filled, unless you agree that we keep it for a future opening, two years at most.
